Trajectory started when I was cleaning out my file cabinet and realized I had years of wellness checks and growth notes for my kids, but no way to visualize trends or compare them over time. I kept thinking about the data I get from [Teslamate](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate) for my car — and how there was nothing like that for my family's health history. We also seem to be sick all the time, so a heatmap sounded fun. That's how Trajectory was born.
