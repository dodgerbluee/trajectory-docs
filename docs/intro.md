# What is Trajectory?

Trajectory is designed for parents and caregivers who self-host their applications. It provides a comprehensive solution for:

- 📈 **Growth Tracking** - Monitor height, weight, head circumference, and other measurements
- 🏥 **Medical Visits** - Record wellness checkups, sick visits, injuries, and vision appointments
- 💉 **Vaccinations** - Keep track of immunizations and schedules
- 🤒 **Illness Tracking** - Log symptoms, medications, and recovery timelines
- 📎 **Document Storage** - Attach medical records, test results, and images
- 👶 **Multiple Children** - Manage health data for your entire family

## Privacy First

Trajectory is built with privacy as a core principle:

- ✅ **Your data stays with you** - All data is stored in your own database and file storage
- ✅ **No phone home** - No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
- ✅ **Data ownership** - You control the data, backups, and access
- ✅ **No third parties** - No external calls except what you configure
