# User Guide

Welcome to the Trajectory User Guide! This guide will walk you through all the features of Trajectory and help you make the most of your family's health tracking.

## What You'll Learn

This guide covers everything you need to know to effectively use Trajectory:

- 🎯 **[First Time Setup](./user-guide/first-time-setup.md)** - Get started with your account and add your first child
- 👶 **[Managing Children](./user-guide/managing-children.md)** - Add, edit, and organize profiles for your children
- 🏥 **[Recording Visits](./user-guide/recording-visits.md)** - Track medical appointments, checkups, and sick visits
- 📈 **[Tracking Growth](./user-guide/tracking-growth.md)** - Record measurements and view growth charts
- 🤒 **[Managing Illnesses](./user-guide/managing-illnesses.md)** - Log symptoms, medications, and track recovery
- 💉 **[Vaccinations & Medical Events](./user-guide/vaccinations-medical-events.md)** - Keep immunization records up to date
- 📎 **[Documents & Attachments](./user-guide/documents-attachments.md)** - Upload and organize medical records
- 👥 **[Family & User Management](./user-guide/family-user-management.md)** - Share access with family members

## Quick Navigation

### New Users
If you're just getting started, begin with the [First Time Setup](./user-guide/first-time-setup.md) guide to create your account and add your first child.

### Daily Use
Most common tasks:
- Recording a doctor's visit → [Recording Visits](./user-guide/recording-visits.md)
- Adding a new measurement → [Tracking Growth](./user-guide/tracking-growth.md)
- Logging an illness → [Managing Illnesses](./user-guide/managing-illnesses.md)
- Uploading a document → [Documents & Attachments](./user-guide/documents-attachments.md)

### Family Management
Learn how to invite family members and manage shared access in the [Family & User Management](./user-guide/family-user-management.md) guide.
