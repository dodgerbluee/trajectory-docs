# First Time Setup

Welcome to Trajectory! This guide will help you set up your account and get started tracking your family's health data.

## Create Your Account

1. Go to your Trajectory URL (for example, `https://trajectory.yourdomain.com`)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Create your account

---

## Adding Your First Child

Now that you have an account, let's add your first child's profile.

1. Click **Add Child**
2. Enter the basics (name, date of birth, sex)
3. Click **Save**

## Start Tracking

Your child’s dashboard shows recent visits, growth trends, and quick actions. If you want, set your preferred measurement units in **Settings**.
