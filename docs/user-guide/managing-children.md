# Managing Children

Learn how to add, edit, and organize profiles for your children in Trajectory.

## Adding a Child

1. Click **Add Child**
2. Enter name, date of birth, and sex
3. Click **Save**

## Editing a Child

Open the child profile, click **Edit**, update details, and **Save**.

---

## Avatars

Add a photo or choose a default avatar from the child profile.

---

## Switching Between Children

Use the child selector in the navigation bar to switch profiles.

---

## Archiving a Child Profile

Use **Archive Profile** in child settings to remove a profile from the active list without deleting data.

---

## Deleting a Child Profile

:::danger Permanent Action
Deleting a child profile is permanent and cannot be undone. All health data, measurements, visits, and documents for that child will be permanently deleted.
:::

If you need to delete a profile, use **Delete Profile** in child settings and confirm the action.

---

## Organizing Multiple Children

Use distinct avatars and consistent names to keep profiles easy to scan.

---

## Access Control

Use **Settings → Family & Access** to restrict which family members can view a child. See [Family & User Management](./family-user-management.md) for details.
