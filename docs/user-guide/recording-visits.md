# Recording Visits

Track all types of medical appointments and visits in Trajectory. Keep a comprehensive record of wellness checkups, sick visits, injuries, and specialist appointments.

## Types of Visits

- 🏥 Wellness, 🤒 Sick, 🚑 Injury, 👁️ Vision, 🦷 Dental, 🏃 Specialty

---

## Adding a Visit

Record a medical visit to keep track of what happened and any important notes.

### Steps to Add a Visit

1. Open the child profile
2. Click **Add Visit**
3. Add type, date, provider, notes, and measurements (optional)
4. Click **Save**

## Deleting a Visit

:::warning Permanent Action
Deleting a visit cannot be undone. Associated measurements and documents may be affected.
:::

To delete a visit:

1. Open the visit record
2. Click **Delete** (usually in the settings or options menu)
3. Confirm the deletion

:::tip Consider Editing Instead
If the visit information is incorrect, consider editing it rather than deleting to maintain the historical record.
:::
