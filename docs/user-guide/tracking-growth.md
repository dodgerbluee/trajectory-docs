# Tracking Growth

Track height, weight, head circumference, BMI, and temperature to see trends over time.

## Add Measurements

1. Open a child profile
2. Click **Add Measurement**
3. Enter type, value, date, and units
4. Click **Save**

## Growth Charts

Go to **Growth** to see percentiles and trends (WHO 0–2, CDC 2–20). Consistency over time matters more than a single point.

## Edit or Delete

Use the **Measurements** list to edit or remove incorrect entries.

## Export

Export charts or CSV data when you need to share records.

### Accessing Measurement History

1. Go to the child's profile
2. Click the **Measurements** tab
3. View all measurements in chronological order
4. Filter by type (height, weight, etc.)
5. Sort by date or value

## Editing Measurements

Correct or update measurement data.

### Editing a Measurement

1. Find the measurement in the list
2. Click **Edit** or the pencil icon
3. Update the value, date, or notes
4. Click **Save Changes**

:::warning Recalculation
Editing measurements will recalculate percentiles and may affect growth chart trends.
:::

---

## Deleting Measurements

Remove incorrect measurements from the record.

### Deleting a Measurement

1. Find the measurement in the list
2. Click **Delete** or the trash icon
3. Confirm the deletion

:::tip Consider Editing
If the value is slightly off, consider editing rather than deleting to maintain the data point on the chart.
:::

---

## Exporting Growth Data

Export measurements and charts for your records or to share with healthcare providers.

### Export Options

1. **CSV Export** - Download measurement data as a spreadsheet
2. **PDF Report** - Generate a printable report with charts
3. **Chart Images** - Save growth charts as PNG/JPG

## Tips for Tracking Growth

✅ **Measure consistently** - Same time of day, same conditions

✅ **Record immediately** - Add measurements right after doctor visits

✅ **Don't obsess over single points** - Focus on the trend over time

✅ **Compare to your child's curve, not others** - Every child grows differently

✅ **Discuss concerns with your pediatrician** - They can interpret the charts in context

✅ **Take photos** - Capture growth milestones with photos attached to measurements
