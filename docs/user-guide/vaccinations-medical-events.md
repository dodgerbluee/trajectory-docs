# Vaccinations & Medical Events

Keep immunizations and major procedures in one place.

## Add a Vaccination

1. Open a child profile
2. Click **Add Vaccination**
3. Enter name, date, and dose
4. Click **Save**

## Vaccination Schedule

Use the **Vaccinations** tab to see what’s completed and what’s due.

## Medical Events

Use **Add Medical Event** for procedures, tests, or hospitalizations.

## Export Records

Export vaccination records for school, travel, or your provider.

Document emergency room visits and hospital stays.

### Recording a Hospitalization

Include:
- **Admission date and time**
- **Discharge date and time**
- **Reason for admission**
- **Diagnosis**
- **Treatment received**
- **Medications given**
- **Length of stay**
- **Follow-up care required**

## Viewing Medical Event Timeline

See all vaccinations and medical events in chronological order.

### Accessing the Timeline

1. Go to the child's profile
2. Click the **Medical Events** or **Timeline** tab
3. View all events chronologically
4. Filter by event type
5. Click any event for full details

## Linking Events to Visits

Connect medical events to doctor visits for context.

### Linking an Event to a Visit

1. When creating/editing a medical event, look for **Related Visit**
2. Select the visit from the dropdown
3. The event will appear in that visit's record

Or from a visit:
1. In the visit form, add medical events
2. Select from existing events or create new

## Reminders for Upcoming Events

Set reminders for vaccine due dates and follow-up appointments.

### Setting a Reminder

1. Open a vaccination or medical event
2. Click **Set Reminder**
3. Choose when to be reminded:
   - 1 week before
   - 1 month before
   - Custom date
4. Save the reminder

## Editing Medical Events

Update event information as needed.

### Editing an Event

1. Open the vaccination or medical event
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update any information
4. Click **Save Changes**

---

## Deleting Medical Events

Remove incorrect records if needed.

### Deleting an Event

1. Open the event
2. Click **Delete** (usually in options menu)
3. Confirm the deletion

:::warning Permanent Action
Deleting a medical event removes it from the timeline and cannot be undone.
:::

## Tips for Tracking Medical Events

✅ **Record immediately** - Add events as soon as they happen

✅ **Keep vaccination cards** - Photo and attach to records

✅ **Note lot numbers** - Helps if there's ever a vaccine recall

✅ **Document reactions** - Even mild ones, for future reference

✅ **Attach documents** - Add test results, discharge summaries, prescriptions

✅ **Link to visits** - Connect events to the visits where they occurred

✅ **Set reminders** - For vaccines due and follow-up appointments
